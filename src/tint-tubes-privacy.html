Privacy policy — Tint Tubes

Short version: the game collects nothing directly. Your progress stays on your device; ads are served by Google AdMob.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

What we collect

Nothing directly. Tint Tubes stores your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) only on your own device. We operate no servers and no accounts.

Advertising

The app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and app-interaction data to serve, personalize, and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy.

On first launch the app asks whether you want personalized or non-personalized ads, and honors that choice. You can reset your device's advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Purchases

The optional remove-ads purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app. The in-app Settings screen always shows the current short version.

About

A small studio with a simple belief.

Business software should be calm: reliable when you depend on it, understandable when you need to change it, and boring in all the right ways.

What we build

CalmDevLabs is a software development studio based in India. We build:

  • Android applications — native apps for customers, field teams, and internal use.
  • Web applications — business platforms, dashboards, and portals.
  • ERP systems — modular business software, starting with an HR portal for employee operations.
  • Backend systems and APIs — the infrastructure that connects everything together.

We also take on IT services work: integrations, database design, and the maintenance and modernization of software you already own.

Engineering philosophy

Four principles guide every project:

  • Solve the real problem. We start from your business need, not from a technology looking for an excuse. If a spreadsheet is the right answer, we will say so.
  • Choose boring technology. Proven tools, well-understood patterns, and dependencies we can justify. Excitement belongs in the product, not in the stack.
  • Build for the next maintainer. Clear code, real documentation, and a handover you can actually use — whether that maintainer is us or your in-house team.
  • Stay honest about trade-offs. Every shortcut has a cost. We name it, price it, and let you decide with open eyes.

How we work with clients

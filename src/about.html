About

A game should be fun in the first minute — or it is not finished. Everything we do starts there: the loop, the feel, the polish, and the testing that proves it on a real phone.

How we think

Five principles guide every game.

Fun first.

We start from the player's first minute, not from a feature list. If the core loop is not fun, nothing around it matters.

Feel is a feature.

Animation, sound, timing, and feedback are not polish added at the end — they are what make a game feel alive, and we treat them as core work.

Tested on real phones.

Emulators do not have thumbs. Every build is played on a real device, the way players will actually play it.

Your code, your game.

Clear code, real documentation, and a handover you can actually use — whether the next maintainer is us or your in-house team. The source code is yours.

Honest about trade-offs.

Every shortcut has a cost. We name it, price it, and let you decide with open eyes — including when the honest answer is "not yet" or "not us."

Small studio, direct line.

We are a young, founder-led studio. You work directly with the people building your game — no account-manager telephone game, no handoffs into the void.

What we will not claim

Facts, not fiction.

You will not find invented founding dates, team bios, client logos, testimonials, awards, downloads, or earnings claims on this site. As the studio grows, this page will grow with it — with facts, not fiction.

How puzzle games get built

The mechanic first. Always.

A puzzle game lives or dies on its first minute. So we prototype the core loop early, tune the difficulty by playtesting, and verify every level is solvable before it ships.

Core loop

One clear action, one clear goal, feedback that feels good. If the loop is not fun in the prototype, we fix the design — not the code around it.

Levels & difficulty

Level packs with a rising curve: gentle starts, real challenges later, and checks that every level can actually be solved.

Reasons to return

Stars, rewards, daily challenges, and progression that give players a reason to open the game tomorrow — without pressure or dark patterns.

How it works

From idea to Play Store.

  1. You send the idea

    Plain words are enough — a paragraph, a sketch, a reference game.

  2. We scope it honestly

    A fixed price, in writing, with what is included and what is not. If it is not a good fit, we say so.

  3. We build in small tested steps

    You see working builds as we go, not a big reveal at the end.

  4. You test it on your phone

    Every build is verified on a real device before we call it done.

  5. Play Store release and handover

    The game, the store listing drafts, and the full source code — yours to keep.

What we will not promise

Earnings. A game's revenue depends on its players, its store listing, and its marketing — nobody honest can guarantee it. We build the game, and we tell you plainly what it can and cannot do.

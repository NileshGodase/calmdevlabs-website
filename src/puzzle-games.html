Before offering to build games for anyone else, we built one for ourselves. Night Pour: Water Sort Puzzle is a liquid sorting puzzle: 200 levels along a winding path, 20 hidden levels, a daily challenge, 13 achievements, a spin wheel, hints and undo — built in small tested steps, 727 tests, every level verified solvable. What we will not promise: earnings. A game's revenue depends on its players, its store listing, and its marketing — nobody honest can guarantee it. We build the game, and we tell you plainly what it can and cannot do.

How it works

From idea to Play Store

  1. You send the idea. Plain words are enough — a paragraph, a sketch, a reference game.
  2. We scope it honestly. A fixed price, in writing, with what is included and what is not. If it is not a good fit, we say so.
  3. We build in small tested steps. You see working builds as we go, not a big reveal at the end.
  4. You test it on your phone. Every build is verified on a real device before we call it done.
  5. Play Store release and handover. The game, the store listing drafts, and the full source code — yours to keep.

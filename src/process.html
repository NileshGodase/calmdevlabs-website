Process

How a game gets built here.

No black boxes. You always know what is being built, why, and what happens next — and you play the game at every stage, not just at the end.

  1. Discovery — the idea, in plain words

    A short conversation, by email or call. You describe the game: what the player does, what makes it fun, games you like as references. We ask questions and play the references. If the idea needs a different shape, we say so now — while changing it is free.

    What we need from you: the idea, however rough. A paragraph or a sketch is enough.

  2. Design & fixed scope — agreed in writing

    Core loop, mechanics, level structure, progression, monetization, and art direction, written down as a scope: features, timeline, and a fixed price, with what is included and what is not. Nothing starts until the scope is agreed.

    What you get: a written plan you can hold us to — and an honest no, if it is not a good fit.

  3. Playable prototype — feel the fun early

    The core loop becomes playable fast, with simple art on purpose. You play it and tell us what feels right and what does not. Design problems are cheapest to fix here, so this is where we are pickiest.

    What you get: a playable build in your hands, plus notes on what works and what needs changing.

  4. Build in small tested steps

    Levels, systems, rewards, and polish land in small steps, each tested as it goes. You see working builds throughout the project — progress is never a mystery, and feedback lands while it is still cheap to act on.

    What you get: regular builds, honest progress notes, and no big reveal at the end.

  5. Playtest on real phones

    Every build is verified on a real device. Difficulty is tuned by playing, levels are checked solvable, and the game is played the way players will play it — on a phone, with one hand, in short sessions.

    What you get: a game that has been played, not just compiled — and your own playtest pass before handover.

  6. Release & handover — the game is yours

    Play Store submission with listing drafts, then the complete source code and build documentation. After launch, new level packs, seasonal content, and fixes are scoped the same honest way — when you want them, not on a retainer you did not ask for.

    What you get: a released game, its source code, and docs your own team can build on.

How we communicate

Short cycles, straight answers.

Builds, not status meetings

You review working software, not slide decks. If a build is late or a design is not working, you hear it from us first.

Honest trade-offs

Every shortcut has a cost. We name it, price it, and let you decide with open eyes — including when the honest answer is "not yet" or "not us."

Direct with the builders

A founder-led studio means you talk to the people building your game. No account-manager telephone game, no handoffs into the void.

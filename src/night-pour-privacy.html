Privacy policy — Night Pour: Water Sort Puzzle

Short version: the game itself collects nothing. Your progress stays on your device; ads are served by Google AdMob, with Meta Audience Network bidding through AdMob mediation.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What we collect

Nothing directly. Night Pour: Water Sort Puzzle stores your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) only on your own device. We operate no servers and no accounts. The game asks you to sign in nowhere, and it does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or financial information.

The game requests no location permission, so it does not collect your precise location. The advertising providers described below may derive an approximate location (such as city-level) from your device's IP address when serving an ad; that is their collection, not ours, and it is described in their policies.

Advertising

Night Pour: Water Sort Puzzle is free and supported by advertising. Ads are served through Google AdMob, which runs a mediation auction in which Meta Audience Network and other mediation partners may bid to show an ad. Two ad formats appear in the game:

  • Rewarded ads — shown only when you choose to watch one, in exchange for a bonus such as an extra spin or an extra hint.
  • Interstitial ads — shown after a level win only, and capped so they do not interrupt gameplay.

What the advertising providers collect

Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, and their mediation partners may collect the following through their software kits (SDKs) built into the app, even though CalmDevLabs itself collects nothing:

  • Advertising ID — the resettable Android advertising ID, and the app set ID.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, screen size, and similar technical details.
  • App interactions — how you interact with ads and with the app around ads, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics — performance information about the app and the ad software, such as app launch time and energy usage.
  • IP address — used to estimate approximate location and to help prevent fraud. A truncated version may be shared with bidding partners.

They use this information to deliver ads, run the mediation auction (deciding which ad to show and how much to bid), prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, including through analytics of ad delivery. Data collected by the ad providers is shared with those providers and, during the auction, with the bidding partners participating in it, as described in their own policies:

  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta's privacy policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy

On first launch the app asks whether you want personalized or non-personalized ads, and honors that choice.

Your choices and opt-outs

  • Device advertising ID: you can reset your device's advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Google ad settings: you can manage how Google personalizes ads to you in your Google account ad settings.
  • Meta ad preferences: you can manage how Meta personalizes ads in your Meta ad preferences.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch a rewarded ad; bonuses tied to them are never required to play the game.

Children's privacy

Night Pour: Water Sort Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, it is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families program, and it does not serve child-directed ads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will address it.

Data retention

Because the game itself collects nothing, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Your game progress lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, and their mediation partners is retained according to their own policies, linked above.

Data deletion requests

The game holds no account and no server-side data about you, so there is no account data for us to delete. To limit or delete the advertising data described above, use the device and provider controls listed under "Your choices and opt-outs" — resetting your advertising ID and adjusting your Google and Meta ad preferences. If you contact us about a deletion request, we will explain what we can and cannot delete and point you to the right provider control.

Purchases

Any optional in-app purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page and will ship with the app. The in-app Settings screen always shows the current short version.

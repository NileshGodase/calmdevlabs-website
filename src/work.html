How we will present work here

When projects ship, each case study will include the problem, the requirements, our technical approach and architecture, honest lessons learned — and outcomes only where they were actually measured. Every entry will be labeled for exactly what it is:

  • Client project — paid engagement, described with the client's permission.
  • Personal project — built by us, on our own time, to solve a real problem.
  • Open source — public code you can inspect yourself.
  • Experiment — a prototype or learning project, labeled as such.

You will never find a learning project dressed up as a client engagement here, or a testimonial nobody wrote.

What we are looking for

Our first client projects will likely be: a focused Android app, a business web application, an HR portal module, or taking over an existing codebase that needs steady hands. If that sounds like your situation, we would like to hear from you — early clients get our full attention and our best work.

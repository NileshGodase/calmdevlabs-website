Our Social Impact

Building Technology. Empowering Futures.

We aim to make a meaningful difference beyond software. CalmDevLabs is committed to dedicating 5% of its profits to initiatives that empower children through education, learning resources, and greater access to opportunity.

At CalmDevLabs, we believe technology should create opportunities beyond business. As the studio grows, a fixed share of what we earn goes toward something we care deeply about: helping children learn.

The commitment is simple and specific — 5% of annual profits (not revenue) set aside for educational access, learning resources, and opportunities for children who need them most. We are a young studio, so this is a commitment we are building toward: our first contributions will be published here, with verified details, when they are made.

CalmDevLabs is a for-profit software studio, not a registered charity. This initiative is a voluntary commitment from our own profits — reported transparently, consistent with our actual accounts.

More about our commitment

Questions about the commitment

What exactly is the 5% commitment?

Each year, we set aside 5% of CalmDevLabs' profits — the money left after the costs of running the studio — for initiatives supporting children's education. It is a share of profits, not of revenue or turnover.

Has CalmDevLabs made any contributions yet?

Not yet. We are a young studio and this commitment is forward-looking. When the first contribution is made, we will publish verified details in the impact updates below.

Is CalmDevLabs a charity or nonprofit?

No. CalmDevLabs is a for-profit software development studio. This is a voluntary commitment from our own profits, not a fundraising campaign — we do not solicit donations.

How will the funds be used, and how will we know?

Funds will go toward educational access, learning resources, and opportunities for children. Every contribution will be reported here with verified details, consistent with our actual accounting records. No donation totals, partner names, or beneficiary stories will appear here until they are real.

Impact updates

No contributions published yet. When we make our first contribution toward children's education, verified details will appear here.

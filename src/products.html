Products & experiments

An empty shelf, on purpose.

Right now our energy goes into client work and getting the studio running. When our own products and experiments ship, they will be listed here — with honest status labels, not launch theater.

How we label product status

  • Concept — an idea we are exploring; nothing built yet.
  • In development — actively being built.
  • Prototype — working, but not production-ready.
  • Released — shipped and usable.

A status only moves when the work behind it is real. No "coming soon" pages for products that do not exist.

What we are exploring

Our interests lean toward practical business tooling — the same domain as our client work: small utilities that remove repetitive work, and components from our ERP efforts (like the HR portal) that could stand alone. When an experiment is worth showing, it will appear here with its code, its status, and its rough edges visible.

For startup programs and partners

If you are evaluating CalmDevLabs for a program or partnership: our product direction is genuine but early. We are a services studio first, building product ideas alongside client work — and we will not pretend otherwise to look further along than we are.

Privacy policy

Short version: this website collects nothing by default. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Nothing, automatically. This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. Simply browsing these pages sends us no personal information.

Contact form

Our project inquiry form does not submit data to any server. When you press send, your browser opens your own email client with a pre-addressed message to us — the information you typed stays with you until you choose to send that email.

If you do email us, we receive whatever you include (name, email address, company, project details) and use it solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Data retention

Inquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up conversation, then deleted on request.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If this website ever adds analytics, a form backend, or any other data collection, this policy will be updated first — and the update date above will change.
